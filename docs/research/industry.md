# UAE Event Staffing — Research Facts (sourced, 2026-09-12)

## 1. Liveforce
Liveforce (liveforce.co) is a UK-founded event/temporary/promotional staffing SaaS platform used by agencies and suppliers to recruit, schedule, and pay temporary and freelance workforces across sectors (events, hospitality, retail, promotional). Staff use the "Crew App" (Android: `co.liveforce.crew`, updated Jul 2026) to view their profile, work calendar, job board, and receive push notifications; staff can apply for jobs and manage timesheets in-app. **It has a real public API**: current docs at `docs.liveforce.co` (GraphQL-based, "Latest changes from 2026-07-21") and `api.liveforce.ai`; an older `liveforce.readme.io` REST/GraphQL doc set is explicitly marked deprecated, telling developers to email `tech@liveforce.co` to request API access. This confirms the chat/job-board model in the brief: agencies post jobs/broadcasts to a job board and staff cannot post back — it's a one-way distribution tool, not a chat product.
Sources: https://liveforce.co/ , https://liveforce.co/staff-scheduling-features/event-staff-app/ , https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=co.liveforce.crew , https://docs.liveforce.co/ , https://api.liveforce.ai/ , https://liveforce.readme.io/reference/authorisation

## 2. The four agencies
- **THA By Armonia UAE** (https://www.armonia-uae.team/) — Dubai-headquartered events/hospitality staffing arm (409 regional staff) of Armonia Group, a French staffing conglomerate (founded 1974, €1.7B revenue, 44K employees, 27 countries); rebranded from "THA Staffing" in 2026 after acquisition. Deployed staff for GISEC Global (visitor management/guest assistance, 33 staff) and the Six Flags Qiddiya opening ceremony (VIP protocol). Sources: armonia-uae.team, linkedin.com/company/tha-by-armonia, informaconnect.com/middle-east-event-show/sponsors/tha
- **Event Lab UAE** (https://www.eventlab.me/) — Dubai staffing/recruitment firm founded 2013, 431 employees, expanded into Saudi in 2016 (80+ KSA clients), added a dedicated Guest Services Staffing department in 2018. Positions itself as bridging freelancers and event agencies across MENA mega-projects. Sources: eventlab.me/about-us, linkedin.com/company/eventlabme, iaapa.org/partners/marketplace/event-lab
- **GIGZ PLUS** (https://gigzplus.com/) — Very new (founded Feb 2025 by CEO Houssam Hussein), Dubai-based, small team (1-10 staff on LinkedIn). Staffs retail activations/brand ambassadors, exhibitions & conferences, corporate events, and catering. Sources: gigzplus.com, gigzplus.com/retail-event-staffing-dubai, linkedin.com/in/houssam-hussein
- **Talent BluePrint UAE** (https://talentblueprint-me.com/) — Dubai staffing firm founded 2019, 72 employees, connects freelance ushers/event professionals ("Join Us" page explicitly recruits "reliable and presentable ushers") to agencies and corporates. Notable case study: staffed the Saudi Arabian Grand Prix (F1) in 2021-2023, deploying 500+ event professionals within three weeks each year. Sources: talentblueprint-me.com/join-us, talentblueprint-me.com/projects/f1, linkedin.com/company/talent-blueprint-fz-lle

## 4. Rates & industry size
- Ushers in Dubai: **AED 50-200/hour**; half-day (4h) **AED 300-800**; full-day (8h) **AED 500-1500**; per-event range AED 300-3000. Source: blogs.gulfgottalents.com/hire-ushers-dubai
- Hostesses/promo staff: standard hostesses **AED 400-700/day**, bilingual **AED 600-1200/day**, promotional models **AED 500-2500/day**, VIP/luxury hostesses **AED 1500-3000+/day**. Source: blogs.gulfgottalents.com/hiring-event-hostesses-and-promotional-staff-in-dubai
- UAE event management market size: **USD 2.62 billion in 2026**, forecast to reach **USD 3.58 billion by 2031** (6.43% CAGR) — Mordor Intelligence (researchandmarkets.com/report/united-arab-emirates-event-management-market). A broader-scope estimate (IMARC) puts the market at **USD 9.6 billion in 2025**, forecast to USD 27.4 billion by 2034.
- UAE's MICE/events sector contributed **AED 100 billion to GDP in 2023** and is linked to **2.3 million jobs** (aggregator stat, treat as directional, not primary). Source: zipdo.co/uae-events-industry-statistics
