Callsheet demo, one take

About ninety seconds. Read the black lines out loud. Do the bold lines. Nothing else.

Before you press record

  1. Open Chrome on the MacBook. Window 1: http://localhost:3100/ (the console). Make it fill the left two thirds of the screen.
  2. Window 2: http://localhost:3100/u/usher-01 (Omar's phone). Make it narrow, like a phone, on the right third. Both windows visible at once, so you never switch.
  3. Ask Claude for a fresh start (say reset). The console should show the headline AI Everything Summit 2026 and a bar at the bottom that says Roster ready.
  4. Press Cmd + Shift + 5. Choose Record Entire Screen. Click Options and pick your microphone. Click Record.
  5. When you finish, click the small stop square in the top menu bar. The video appears on the desktop.

The script

1
Console

Point at the grey request line at the top.

"This is what a staffing coordinator types. One line. Sixty ushers, two days, six areas, the rate, the training."

2
Console

Point at the big headline and the line under it that says Venue verified.

"Callsheet researched the event on the web with Exa. It verified the venue against five sources and found the organiser on its own."

3
Console

Point at the progress bar with the words Research, Crew, Verify, Roster, Approve.

"It searched its own CRM for crew, checked that their past events really happened, and built the roster. Ten per cent of every area is held for first-timers, so newcomers get a real chance."

4
Console

Scroll down to the rows of name chips. Point at the bar at the bottom that says Roster ready. Do not click yet.

"Sixty seats proposed. Nothing has been sent. This is the human gate: the agent does the work, a person makes the call."

5
Console

Click the red button Approve and send offers. Wait three seconds.

"One tap. Offers go out from the agent's own mailbox in Ambiguous, and a task is created for every confirmation it is waiting on."

6
Phone window

Point at the offer on Omar's phone, then click I'm in.

"Omar gets the whole offer: dates, hours, rate, training, transport, and the exact position he was matched to. He taps I'm in. That is the entire application."

7
Console

Look at the Stage row. Omar's chip has turned green. Point at the Live feed on the right.

"His seat fills, and a training reminder is created in the agent's task list, automatically."

8
Console

Say the word replies to Claude (or type scripts/demo.sh replies 23 in a terminal off screen). Watch the big number climb.

"Replies land. A decline promotes the next name off the waitlist. The call sheet writes itself."

9
Console

In the Agent workspace card on the right, click Open call sheet. Show the document.

"Callsheet is a registered coworker in Ambiguous, with its own mailbox, its own CRM, its own tasks, and this document. Sixty ushers, placed on verified experience, in minutes. Not by wasta, over weeks."

If something looks wrong: stop recording, say reset to Claude, wait ten seconds, start again. Every take is a fresh run.
Honesty line if a judge asks: the 23 replies in step 8 are a simulation for the demo and the log says so; Omar's reply, the emails, the tasks and the document are real.
