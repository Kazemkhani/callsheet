# Motion-graphic opener prompt (paste into the code-and-math motion tool, with the GPT image attached)

This chat is for creating videos as smooth as motion graphics with code and math.

Hermes the asset animator: build a 12-second, 16:9, 60fps opener for a product called Callsheet.
Attached is the hero still (phone in hand, backstage, Telegram group with an offer card). Use it
as the base plate for the last 4 seconds. Firstly, write a plan for what you will execute and
how, then wait for my approval before rendering.

VOICEOVER (high quality, calm, confident male or female voice, British English, no music bed
louder than -18 LUFS, VO at -14 LUFS). It must start exactly with these three lines, no "have
you ever":

  "Four agencies. Four profiles. One month waiting for an interview."
  "Job posts you can't reply to."
  "Hired by who you know, not what you've done."

Then, after a 400 ms beat:

  "Meet Callsheet. The staffing coordinator that lives in your crew's group chat,
   and runs its own office."

VISUALS, timed to the VO:
- 0.0s  Paper background (#F4F1EA), subtle film grain, ink type (#1A1917). Editorial serif
        headline face for the big words, a clean grotesk for small text. No gradients, no glow,
        no purple, no sparkles, no rainbow.
- 0.2s  As line 1 is spoken, a mobile profile form pops up centre-screen (the real thing:
        "Your Details" checklist with green ticks: Name, Avatar, Date of Birth, Mobile Number,
        Email, Address). Camera pushes in smoothly (ease-out cubic, 600 ms) to a 130% zoom on it.
        Then three more identical forms slam in behind it, stacked and offset, each with a
        different agency logo tile (THA, Event Lab, GIGZ, Talent Blueprint). A small counter
        ticks "Day 1 ... Day 31" in tabular mono digits in the corner, fast, with soft clicks.
- 3.4s  Camera glides DOWN (same easing, 700 ms) to a second card: a one-way broadcast post
        ("** New message from Event Lab UAE ** We are looking for Event Staff ...  View job") with
        a greyed-out, disabled reply box. Zoom to 130%. The cursor tries to type; every keypress
        is a satisfying mechanical click (thock, OpenAI-launch-video quality, tight, no reverb)
        but nothing appears in the box. The box shakes 4 px twice.
- 6.2s  Camera glides DOWN again to a third card: a phone call UI "Incoming: Supervisor"
        with a caption "Omar, we need you on this event" and a small label "known contact".
        Next to it, a greyed row of 40 tiny avatars labelled "qualified, never called".
        Zoom to 130%, hold 900 ms.
- 8.4s  Hard cut to black for 6 frames. Then the hero still fades up (400 ms) with a slow
        2% push-in. The Telegram offer card on the phone gets a subtle highlight sweep. The
        buttons "I'm in" pulse once (scale 1.00 -> 1.04 -> 1.00, 300 ms). Three reply bubbles
        pop in one after another (120 ms apart) with a soft message "tick" sound each.
- 11.0s The wordmark "Callsheet" sets in the serif at 120 px, ink on paper, letter-spaced
        tight, with a single signal-orange (#C8401F) underline drawing left to right (500 ms).
        Under it, 14 px caps: "AGENTS, EVERYWHERE. EVEN BACKSTAGE."
- 12.0s Freeze on the wordmark for the hand-off to the screen recording.

SOUND: every UI pop is a short, dry, satisfying click (keyboard thock family, 40-70 ms,
slightly varied pitch so it never sounds copied). Camera moves have a faint air whoosh at
-30 LUFS. No cheesy risers. End with room tone, not silence.

MOTION RULES: physical easing only (ease-out cubic or spring, damping 0.8), nothing linear,
nothing bouncing for decoration; every move exists to show what the VO is saying at that
moment. Zoom targets are the exact cards being described. Respect a 60 to 80 character
measure on any paragraph text. Export: 3840x2160 ProRes 422 + a 1920x1080 H.264 preview.

Deliver: the plan first, then a 12-second render, then the separate VO WAV and click SFX stems.
